Strength Rebuild — beautification

The three Forte moves you liked — the rest clock that fills like a pill, the pressed sprig, the softened boxes — translated into graphite and brass instead of copied. All three options below include the new rest treatment; they differ in the ornament and the box language. Same bones, nothing moves.

Option 1

Pressed oak

The companion piece to Carolina's rose: a hand-drawn oak twig with a pair of acorns where her sprig carries a blossom — oak for strength, drawn in brass, tucked behind the cards. Boxes take the tucked corner too (16/16/16/5), the forge-cut sibling of her petal.

Where: oak twig pressed into the open bottom corner on home, beside the header on workout · tucked corner on every card and the rest pill.

Option 2

Blueprint

Rebuild, read literally: the screen as a drafting sheet. Crop-mark brackets and a small brass register cross sit in the header corner, a dashed dimension line underlines the header, and every card becomes a cut plate — one corner chamfered at 45° instead of rounded. The most architectural of the three.

Where: crop marks + register cross in the header · dimension hairline under the topbar · chamfered corner on cards and the rest pill.

Option 3

Knurl

Ornament from the bar itself. A thin diamond-knurl band runs under the header — the same cross-hatch you grip every session — and the rest clock fills with knurling instead of a flat wash, so the texture literally builds as you rest. Cards machine down to a tighter radius with a milled top edge. No pictures, all texture.

Where: knurl hem under every header · knurled rest fill · radius 8 + milled edge on cards.

The filling rest pill ships with whichever option you pick — it's the shared upgrade. All three survive the light (paper + bronze) theme: the oak and knurl pick up the deeper bronze, the blueprint marks read like pencil on paper. And everything here is inline SVG or CSS — no new assets, no cache changes beyond the version bump.
