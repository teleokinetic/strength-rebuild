Kept from Forte's Progresso:

  • The three moments in the same order — arcs, then the gym trends, then rhythm — and the quiet entry row on home with a live one-line pulse.
  • Ladder rail with dates earned, one now dot, goal on top. SR has two arcs ready-made: the muscle-up pips and the Nordic rungs — both already live data, no new upkeep.
  • Trend card = "first → last · delta" + a bare sparkline. No axes, no grid — the two numbers are the axis.

SR-native (what Forte can't say):

  • Step lines, not slopes. A working weight holds, then jumps 5. The plateau is the double-progression stair — a smooth slope would be a lie.
  • The mechanism line. Each card ends with the scheme's verdict, computed from chip weight + reps vs the slot's range: at ×8 — load 65 next (brass = act now) · build reps 9 → 10 · fixed-rep lifts get cadence (bumps every ~2 sessions) · stalls echo the slot's own cue.
  • Goal ticks from the board. Squat's chart runs to 230 — the headroom is drawn, not implied. Only board lifts get one.
  • Two tiers. Board lifts get full cards; assistance folds to hairline rows with mini-sparks. ▲ = at top of range, loads next. Eleven lifts don't deserve eleven hero cards.
  • Counts, not streaks. "14 sessions · about 2 a week" — facts, no chains to break (the Still Water rule). Week pips reuse the trail's tucked-corner pip.
  • One barley per surface — it closes the page; goal rungs get a plain brass ring instead of Forte's bloom.

Deliberately out:

  • Volume, tonnage, e1RM, muscle heat-maps — there is no per-set data, and fake precision would poison the instrument.
  • Per-lift history screens — deep analysis belongs to the export at recalibration time.

The board stays the where you stand instrument; this page is how it's moving. They share data, goals, and the recal date — the page just gives the board's numbers their time axis.
