The calm pass:

  • One surface, one pattern. Arcs and lifts are all hairline rows — the exact collapsed→expanded move the Targets board already taught. Resting state fits one screen, no scroll.
  • Brass budget. Collapsed, brass appears only as ▲ (at top of range — loads next). Sparks sit dim until a row opens; then the one open card gets the brass line, the goal tick, and the mechanism verdict.
  • Every row still answers the question — current number, reps position or delta, and the tiny trace — without asking to be read.

Cut from round 1:

  • The rhythm card — folded to five words in the header sub.
  • The hero-card tier and the two-tier split — no lift outranks another until you tap it.
  • Rail captions, baseline descriptions, the duplicate chin-up trend card (the arc owns chins).

Muscle-up arc, granular: ten rungs instead of four stations, grouped by phase tags on the rail — Pull → Grip → Rings → Transition → the gate. Uniform one-line rungs: name left, target or date right; only the live rung speaks. The three real lanes (pull strength, false grip, ring strength) are deliberately flattened into one editable column so the rail stays quiet — and like the Nordic ladder, the rungs are program data, not code: these ten are a strawman for you to rename, insert between, or reorder in the editor.
