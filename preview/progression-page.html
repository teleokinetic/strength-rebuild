What round 3 changes:

  • The board owns position; this page owns motion. The same seven names can appear on both — different question. Everything here carries time: a journey (165 → 195), a window (8 wks), a tenure (5 sessions), a pace.
  • One taxonomy: motion. Next session / climbing / holding — arcs and lifts sort together, and the grouping is the reading. Nordic sitting in Holding isn't a failure banner; it's the page noticing three weeks on rung 4 so you don't have to.
  • Verdicts lead. Round 2's ▲ chips become the top section — the only brass on the resting page. Ready rows drop their spark; the pill is the row's point.
  • Every open card ends in a verdict. Ready → the load. Climbing → pace and a soft ETA in dim ink (straight-line guess; recalibration corrects it). Holding → a reset option, hairline not brass — it's a suggestion, not a command.

Cut from round 2:

  • The Arcs / In-the-gym split — that's a kind split, position thinking.
  • Current-number rows (195 · +30) — journeys instead: 165 → 195.
  • The ▲ chip — promoted from footnote to the first section.

The sections are data, not curation: ready = logged at the top of the rep range; holding = three sessions without a weight, rep, or rung change; everything else is climbing, sorted by last bump. Rows move between sections on their own as sessions land.
