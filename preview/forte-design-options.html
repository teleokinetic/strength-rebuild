Forte — three directions

Forte is Portuguese for strong. Same bones as Strength Rebuild — two day cards and a targets panel at home, one-press rest and weight chips in session — with a gentler, friendlier skin. React to palette, type, shape, and voice separately; mixing across options is a fine answer.

Option 1
Morning Rosé

Soft warmth. Rounded type, petal-cornered day cards, a greeting up top. The rest timer is a pill that drains as she rests — the whole button is the clock.
