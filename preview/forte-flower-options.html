Forte — a little flora

Three ways to let a flower into Morning Rosé. The bones stay exactly the same — this is ornament only. The flower is a five-petal wild rose drawn from the same deep rose as the buttons, so it reads as part of the palette, not a sticker on top.

Option 1

Pressed sprig

A hand-drawn rose sprig tucked beside the greeting, like a flower pressed into a notebook. It appears once per screen, in the empty corner, and never touches the content. The quietest of the three.

Where: beside the greeting on home · header corner on the workout page. Everything else unchanged.

Option 2

Bloom when done

The flower is earned, not printed. A finished exercise gets a blossom instead of a checkmark, the current one shows a faint bud, and the week's finished sessions collect beside the greeting. A fresh screen has almost no flowers — by the end of a workout, it's in bloom.

Where: done-marks on exercise cards · a small “this week” row on home · a full bloom on the session-done banner.

Option 3

Petal hem

A row of tiny petals under the header, like embroidered trim on linen, plus one or two pale petals drifting behind the cards. Always present on every screen, but faint enough to read as texture rather than decoration.

Where: a hem under every screen's header · faint background petals. Cards and buttons untouched.

All three carry over to evening rosé (dark mode) as-is — the blossom just picks up the lighter rose. Mixing is also fine: the sprig on home plus bloom-marks for done exercises is a natural pair. None of these add assets — every flower is a few lines of inline SVG.
